OUR STORY

More than coffee. A place to belong.

At Caffinity, coffee is where the moment starts. Connection is what makes it matter.

THE BEGINNING · 2024

We noticed something about cafés.

Around 2024 we started to see a pattern in the cafés we went to. You went for the coffee, but you tended to stay for something else.

A chat with a mate. A study session which would invariably turn into a bit of laughter. A new idea being worked through and shared over a table. Or just a bit of time away from the normality of it all.

We started asking ourselves a question:

What if we created a café where connection was as important as coffee?
THE IDEA

Not another drinks and snacks place.

The idea wasn't to build another drinks and snacks place. It was to create an atmosphere in which people could just be themselves.

A place where students could come and talk, where friends could come and make memories, and where a simple coffee break could end up being the best day of someone's life.

THE NAME

CAFFINITY

Caffinity came from combining “Café” and “Affinity”, the sense of togetherness, belonging and connection.

By 2025, the concept had matured into a clear identity: a café designed to welcome people, stand out and bring people together.
